# AcePharm Copy & Tone Compliance Verification Matrix

This matrix serves as a development checklist to ensure that all user-facing copy in `apps/marketing`, `apps/web`, and email templates strictly adheres to the editorial principles set forth in **Website Copy v2.0** (`WEBSITE-COPY-EXTRACTED.md`) and the **Developer Brief v2.1**.

---

## 1. Banned Phrases Checklist

The following phrases are **strictly prohibited** anywhere in the product:

| Prohibited Phrase / Pattern | Why It Is Banned | Compliant Alternative | Status in Marketing App |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **“You failed”** | Demoralising, counterproductive to calm evidence-led study. | *“Needs review”*, *“3 of 10 correct”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **“You are falling behind”** | Anxiety-inducing false urgency. | *“Recommended next session: [Topic]”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **“Only [X] days left” / “Don't lose your streak”** | Gamified stress triggers. | *“Current streak: [X] study days”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **“We miss you!”** | Manipulative engagement copy. | Standard calm notification of scheduled revision. | ✅ Compliant |
| **“Wrong!”** | Harsh; lacks clinical insight. | Highlight selected option with distractor rationale. | ✅ Compliant |
| **“Mastered”** | Medically imprecise; suggests no future review is required. | *“High confidence & consistent accuracy”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **“Guaranteed” / “Pass guaranteed”** | Unverifiable claim; breaches educational integrity. | *“Evidence-led revision designed for UK pharmacy”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **“Complete curriculum coverage”** | Medically unbounded claim. | *“High-yield topics mapped to GPhC frameworks”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **Unauthorised GPhC / NHS Endorsement Claims** | Regulatory breach. | Prominent independence disclaimer on every page. | ✅ Compliant |
| **“AI-powered” as standalone selling point** | Buzzword; explains nothing about student utility. | Describe what it does: *“Trained on reviewed UK rationales”* | ✅ Compliant |
| **Implying Ace has independent clinical authority** | Clinical safety risk. | Explicit disclaimer that Ace is an educational tutor. | ✅ Compliant |

---

## 2. British English & Clinical Conventions

- **Spelling:** British English throughout (*organise, personalised, analyse, programme, paediatric*).
- **Terminology:** Use **“medicines”** (never *“meds”*).
- **Units of Measure:** Write **“micrograms”** in full (never *“mcg”* or *“µg”* to prevent medication error misinterpretation).
- **Time & Dates:** 12-hour format with lower case am/pm (e.g., *“3:00 pm”*); dates formatted as *“18 September 2026”*.

---

## 3. Mandatory Regulatory Disclaimers

The following disclaimers must be permanently rendered in the footer of all public and authenticated views:

1. **Independence Disclaimer:**
   > *“AcePharm is an independent educational platform. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the General Pharmaceutical Council, universities, NICE, the NHS or any other organisation unless explicitly stated.”*

2. **Educational Disclaimer:**
   > *“AcePharm is an educational resource. It does not provide patient-specific medical advice and does not replace official guidance, university teaching, professional supervision, clinical judgement or local policies.”*
